# Code of conduct

We want uidx to be a welcoming project for people with different backgrounds
and levels of experience. This applies to issues, pull requests, reviews and
other project spaces.

- Be respectful and patient. Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Give constructive feedback and assume good faith while addressing harm.
- Respect people's privacy, identity and boundaries.
- Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, share private information, or post
  sexual content or unwanted attention.

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close discussions, or restrict
participation when needed to protect the community. Responses should account
for the impact and severity of the behavior and any repeated violations.

Report conduct concerns privately to [the maintainer](https://github.com/beharguy)
using an available private contact method. If none is available, open an issue
requesting a private contact without including names or sensitive details.
Do not post private reports in public issue or pull-request discussions.
